## About Me

I am a systems architect and optimization researcher at Huawei, where I am the Tech Lead of the Communication Services Group. I work on reliable and efficient end-to-end communication systems spanning remote cloud-to-device communication over Wi-Fi and cellular networks, device-to-device connectivity over Bluetooth and NearLink, and satellite networks. My technical interests include (1) low-bandwidth communication and transport optimization for both device-to-device and device-to-cloud links, and (2) low-power communication through software, hardware, and chipset co-design.

I joined Huawei through the [TopMinds Program](https://career.huawei.com/cn/young-genius) in 2024. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (Yao Class), Tsinghua University](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where my research focused on optimization, control, and game theory for large-scale networked systems. I received my B.Eng. from the [Department of Electronic Engineering](https://www.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/index.htm) and [Xinya College](https://www.xyc.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), with a minor in Economics from the [School of Economics and Management](https://www.sem.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).

## Research
{: #research }

My research focuses on optimization, control, and game-theoretic algorithms for large-scale, uncertain, and complex networked systems, with applications in communication networks, computing systems, energy storage, and smart grids.

### Research Areas

#### Large-Scale Coordination via Mean-Field and Aggregative Games
{: .research-heading }

<p class="research-period">October 2022 – October 2023</p>

I studied control and coordination in large-scale multi-agent systems, including mean-field games with an asymptotically large population and aggregative games under communication and bandwidth constraints. This work covers equilibrium analysis in vehicle-to-grid systems, dynamic programming, G-prox PDHG, and privacy-preserving coordination algorithms.

#### Smoothed Online Convex Optimization
{: .research-heading }

<p class="research-period">October 2021 – September 2022</p>

I studied online decision-making under uncertainty when both unknown hitting costs and switching costs are present. I developed algorithms for different norm settings and explored applications in data-center scheduling, content delivery and caching, power-system restoration, and continual learning.

#### Nonconvex Energy Storage Scheduling and Carbon Reduction
{: .research-heading }

<p class="research-period">August 2020 – September 2021</p>

I studied the trade-off between economic and environmental costs in power systems and developed nonconvex storage-scheduling algorithms that improve computational efficiency while retaining convergence guarantees.
